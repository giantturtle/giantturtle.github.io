Spotlight for Android

Stop scrolling through app drawers and cluttered menus. Macro Spotlight brings the power of desktop-class search to your Android device, inspired by the best of GNOME and iOS.

Total Privacy: This app has no internet permissions. Your search history and data never leave your device.

Lightning Fast: Built for speed. Find apps, contacts, and files with a few keystrokes.

Home Screen Widget: A clean, minimal search bar for your home screen that respects your privacy.

Quick Settings: Swipe down from the top of your screen and add our Tile. Now you can search even while you're inside other apps.

Unit & Currency: Convert money or measurements on the fly—completely offline.
