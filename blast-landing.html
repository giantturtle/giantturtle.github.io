Blast Liquid Logic
Ready to make a splash? Dive into the colorful world of Blast Liquid Logic, the puzzle game that combines satisfying gameplay with brain-teasing strategy!
Your goal is simple: Tap groups of matching liquid blocks to pop them and clear the screen. But don't be fooled by the colorful slime - you’ll need to think ahead to make sure you don't run out of moves!
Why you’ll love Blast Liquid Logic:
Satisfying Popping Action: Tap matching colors and watch them splash away!
Brain-Teasing Levels: clear the tiles strategically to advance to the next stage.
Helpful Power-Ups: Stuck on a tricky block? Use the Extra Shot to break any single block!
Vibrant Graphics: Enjoy high-quality, gooey graphics and smooth animations.

✨ Key Features:

🧩 Addictive Puzzles: Simple to learn, challenging to master.
🎨 Colorful Liquid Theme: Bright, vibrant slime and goo aesthetics.
🎯 Targeted Power-ups: Use the target booster to clear specific tiles.
🏆 Level Progression: Track your tiles and level up.

Puzzle, Pop, Blast, Match, Slime, Logic, Brain Teaser, Casual, Tap Game, Cube, Block Puzzle.

The more tiles you pop at once, the higher your score. With vibrant colors and smooth animations, Blast offers a relaxing yet intellectually stimulating experience for puzzle lovers of all ages.
