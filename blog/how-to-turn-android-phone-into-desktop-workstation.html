01Why Stock Android & DeX Leave Most Phones Behind

Modern Android phones ship with 8-core Snapdragon, Tensor, or Dimensity silicon and 8–16GB of fast RAM—plenty for writing, spreadsheets, coding, and research. Yet plugging a standard phone into a monitor hits three immediate walls:

Black Pillar Bars

Standard screen mirroring projects a narrow 9:16 phone strip onto a 16:9 monitor, wasting over half the display.

Stretched Launchers

Rotating stock launchers stretches portrait widgets awkwardly and gives you no desktop taskbar or mouse-friendly switcher.

Locked Ecosystems

Samsung DeX and Moto Ready For are locked to flagship models—leaving Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, and budget users out.

07Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a Samsung DeX experience on Google Pixel, Xiaomi, or Motorola?

Yes. While Samsung DeX is locked to high-end Galaxy phones, Dextop works across Pixel, Xiaomi, Motorola, OnePlus, Nothing Phone, and virtually any Android smartphone or tablet.

Do I need an expensive USB-C dock or monitor to use Dextop?

No. Dextop works standalone directly on your phone, tablet, or foldable screen. To connect an external monitor, an inexpensive $15–$25 USB-C to HDMI adapter is all you need.

Does Dextop require root access or ADB commands?

No. Dextop installs like any standard app from Google Play—zero root privileges, bootloader unlocks, or developer scripts required.

Will Dextop mess up my existing portrait home screen?

No. Dextop maintains independent landscape desktop shortcuts, leaving your standard portrait home screen icons, widgets, and wallpaper untouched.
