## 1. The Universal "Photo Sharing Anxiety"
Handing an unlocked smartphone to someone to show a single photo or meme often results in immediate awkwardness: their thumb instinctively swipes left or right, exposing personal messages, private screenshots, or sensitive albums.

## 2. Why Android App Pinning Falls Short
Android's native "App Pinning" feature locks the phone inside the app, but **touch gestures within the app remain fully active**. Anyone can still browse your entire photo gallery, delete photos, or open private albums.
