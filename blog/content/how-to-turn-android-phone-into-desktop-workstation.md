## 2. Why Default Android Fails at Desktop Multitasking
When you connect a standard Android phone to a computer monitor or TV:
- **Black Pillar Bars:** Mirroring vertical (9:16) phone screens onto 16:9 displays wastes 60% of screen real estate.
- **Stretched Icons:** Standard launchers stretch mobile icons horizontally, making navigation clumsy.
- **Missing Desktop Paradigms:** There is no persistent taskbar, independent desktop shortcuts, or desktop-style app switching.
