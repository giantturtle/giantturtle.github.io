Privacy Policy for Paste Box

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Paste Box is committed to your privacy.

2. Your data stays on your device:

    Paste Box does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All clipboard history and scratchpad content are stored locally on your device.

3. No Third Parties:

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

4. Permissions:

The app requires access to the system clipboard to function as a clipboard manager. This data always remains on your computer.

5. Sandboxing:

The app operates within the macOS Sandbox to ensure your system's security.
