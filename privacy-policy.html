Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Welcome to GiantTurtle Software ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to our macOS applications—including Desktops / DesktopPlus and Zones&Corners—as well as our website (https://giant-turtle.com).

Our core philosophy is simple: we do not collect, track, or share your personal data.

1.Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or require any personal information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number) inside DesktopPlus or Zones&Corners.
  • No Telemetry or Usage Tracking: Our macOS apps do not collect analytics, behavioral statistics, usage telemetry, or crash reports.
  • No Accounts Required: You do not need to create an account to use our applications, and we do not track your activity across sessions, apps, or websites.

2.On-Device Processing & Local Storage

  • Local Execution: All core features—including active desktop monitoring, workspace switching, app management, screen corner/edge zone detection, Apple Vision OCR text extraction, screen color picking, clipboard counts, and customizable styles or overlays—execute locally on your Mac.
  • Local Preferences & History: Configuration files, ignored application lists, recent OCR extractions, recent color swatches, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your Mac (in macOS UserDefaults or local Application Support folders) and are never uploaded to our servers.

3.macOS System Permissions

Depending on the features you use, DesktopPlus and Zones&Corners may request macOS permissions strictly for local functionality:

  • Accessibility: Used in real time on your Mac for window/space switching, cursor corner/edge detection, window management, and simulating user-configured hotkeys.
  • Screen Recording (when prompted): Used strictly locally when performing screen captures, OCR text extraction, color picking, or space/window previews.
  • Automation / Apple Events (when prompted): Used only when you configure actions that interact with apps or system controls (such as AppleScripts, Safari, Apple Music, or Finder).
  • No captured screen content, OCR text, window titles, or keystrokes are recorded remotely, stored on our servers, or shared with third parties.

4.Third-Party Services & Payment Processing

  • No Third-Party Trackers: DesktopPlus and Zones&Corners do not bundle third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK).
  • Payment Processing: If you purchase a license, checkout is handled by secure third-party payment processors (such as Dodo Payments). We do not store or have access to your full credit card or banking details.

5.Network Activity

DesktopPlus and Zones&Corners operate offline for everyday local features. Network requests are only used for:

  • License Verification: Activating, validating, or deactivating a purchase/license key using non-personal activation identifiers (such as a masked/hashed license key and instance ID).
  • User-Triggered Actions & Update Checks: Optional update checks where enabled, or actions you explicitly configure to access the network (such as Network Speed Test, Open a Website, or a custom script/Terminal command).

6.Children's Privacy

Because our applications do not collect any personal data, they do not knowingly collect or solicit information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7.Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
