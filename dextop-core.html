Full Desktop Ergonomics on OEM-Restricted Phones

Dextop Core is a dedicated edition of Dextop without home-screen launcher capabilities, created because some device manufacturers restrict third-party launchers or disable full-screen gestures when you switch launchers.

Even if your phone supports landscape orientation, Dextop Core gives you a completely separate horizontal workspace with custom themes, widgets, and shortcuts tailored for productivity and widescreen entertainment.

Pro Tip: Check your Android Developer Options to see if windowed mode is available for an even richer multi-window desktop experience.
