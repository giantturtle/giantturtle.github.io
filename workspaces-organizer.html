Workspaces Organizer

Linux
Open Source

A smarter way to navigate GNOME workspaces.

Seamless workspace management for your Linux desktop

Workspaces Organizer is a powerful GNOME Shell extension that replaces the default workspace indicator. It provides a horizontal view of your workspaces, instantly showing you which apps are open in each space, making multitasking fluid and intuitive.

Visual App Indicators

Instantly see the icons of opened applications in every workspace, keeping you oriented at all times.

Scroll to Switch

Quickly jump between workspaces by simply scrolling over the indicator in your top bar.

Drag-and-Drop

Easily organize your open windows by dragging and dropping them into a different workspace directly from the indicator.

Reliable Base

A robust fork of the popular Workspace Indicator by fmuellner, enhanced with more visual details and interaction capabilities.

Enhance your Linux workflow with better visibility and control over your desktop environment.
